Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026 · Last updated 13 October 2026

The short version
  • TrimLabel is free. There are no accounts, no logins and no sign-ups.
  • Your PDFs are processed on your own device. They are never uploaded to us.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.
  • We keep nothing about you, because we never receive anything.

This policy explains how TrimLabel (the website and the Android app, together "TrimLabel", "we", "us") handles information. It is operated by [OPERATOR NAME]. "You" means anyone who uses TrimLabel.

1. What we collect

We do not collect personal information. TrimLabel does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, address, or payment details, and it has no account system.

The shipping label PDFs you add are read and processed inside your browser or on your phone. They are not sent to our servers, to any cloud service, or to any third party by TrimLabel.

2. What is stored on your device

To remember your choices, TrimLabel saves a small amount of data in your browser's local storage (website) or in the app's own storage (Android). This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

DataWhat it containsWhy
SettingsYour default layout, whether cut marks are on, and whether auto-detect is onSo the app opens the way you left it
Label layoutsThe crop areas used to trim each marketplace's labelsTo trim labels correctly
ActivityDate and time, marketplace name, number of files, and output type of your last 50 print jobs. No file names and no file contents.To show your recent activity

You can erase all of it at any time: use "Clear History" in the Activity page, clear your browser's site data for TrimLabel, or uninstall the Android app. We do not set cookies.

3. Website hosting

The website is delivered by a hosting provider (currently Vercel). Like almost every website, the host may automatically record technical request data such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested, and the time. This is used to run and secure the service. We do not use this data to identify you, and we do not combine it with anything else.

TrimLabel loads everything it needs (code, fonts, icons) from its own domain. It does not call advertising networks, analytics services, social media widgets, or other third-party content.

4. Android app

The Android app works offline. It requests only the Internet permission, which it uses to check for app updates. Update files and update information are hosted on GitHub, so GitHub may see your IP address and standard request details when the app checks for an update. The app does not send your files or your usage data when it does this.

The app asks for no access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos.

5. Sharing and exports

When you create a print file, TrimLabel saves it to your device or opens your phone's share menu. Where it goes after that is your choice (for example, Google Drive, WhatsApp, or a printer). Those services have their own privacy policies and we have no control over them.

6. Your customers' data

Shipping labels usually contain your customers' names, addresses, and phone numbers. TrimLabel does not receive this data, but you remain responsible for handling it lawfully, for example by keeping printed labels and exported files secure and disposing of them properly.

7. Children

TrimLabel is a business tool and is not directed at children under 18. Because we collect no personal information, we hold no data about any user, including children.

8. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal information about you, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. If you believe we hold information about you, or you have a privacy question or complaint, contact us (see below) and we will respond promptly. Where applicable law, such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, gives you rights, we will honour them.

9. Security

Processing happens locally, which keeps your files off the network. The website is served over HTTPS and uses a strict content security policy so that it only runs its own code. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your device and its operating system up to date.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will say so inside the app or on the home page.
