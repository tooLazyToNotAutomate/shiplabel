    About TrimLabel

    Small sellers deserve big-business tools.

    Behind every parcel is a person who packed it, often late at night, often by themselves. We built TrimLabel so that the paperwork never slows them down.

    Free. For everyone. For good. No price tag, no trial, no login, no email. TrimLabel is a free tool for the people who keep online shopping moving.
    Our mission

    To take the hassle out of shipping, so every seller can spend their time on what they love: their products and their customers.

    What we believe
    The same quality for everyone The tool that helps a home business should be as good as the one a warehouse uses.
    Your data is yours Your orders and customer details stay on your device. We never see them.
    Simple wins If it takes more than three steps, we haven't finished designing it.
    Who we're here for
    Home-based sellers Who pack orders at the kitchen table.
    Small shops Growing one order at a time.
    Packing teams Who need hundreds of labels done before the courier arrives.
